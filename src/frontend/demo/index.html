Architecture principles in the MVP

  • Human authority: risk tier is advisory.
  • Explainability: high-risk factors are retained.
  • Least privilege: review actions require reviewer/admin role.
  • Auditability: submissions and decisions create events.
  • Privacy: demo data is synthetic and browser-local.
